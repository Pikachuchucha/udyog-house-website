Working tool — local only, no data sent, no match shown

Check a brief in general terms. Get the questions, not a fake match.

No directory exists yet on this site, so this tool shows no supplier, no price, and no lead time. What it does show is what to ask, and what evidence to request, for the kind of job you describe — in general terms only.

For a cnc machining job in stainless steel, at precision (±0.02–0.05 mm)

Ask first

  • Do you regularly work in stainless steel for cnc machining — or would this be a first?
  • What tolerance have you actually held on a comparable job, and what record shows it?
  • What would stop this job from fitting your current capacity, in plain terms?

Evidence to request — and what each level does not prove

  • P0 Supplier-declared: the shop's own words. Useful as a starting point; not a check.
  • P1 Document received: a document was received and logged. Receipt is not authenticity, currency, or capability.
  • P2 Cross-checked: named fields matched a named source on a named date. Identity/document only.
  • P3 Observed capability: capability actually seen, by a stated method, at a stated time. Not an ongoing quality guarantee.

Do not send yet

Do not send drawings or confidential specifications through this site, or through this tool. Describe the need in general terms first, and share drawings only through a direct, agreed channel once a shop and a scope are clear.
