01

Purpose

Make industrial discovery structured, transparent, and accessible

Udyog House is building India's industrial discovery and sourcing network, beginning in Maharashtra. Its purpose is to make industrial discovery and sourcing more structured, transparent, and accessible — starting with structured capability profiles for tool rooms, mould makers and precision machining businesses.

Principles

  • Exact, plain-spoken, rooted, builder-minded, fair.
  • Owner-approved information, with evidence and limits stated.
  • No invented counts, members, testimonials, logos, partnerships, or revenue.
  • No guarantee of enquiries, orders, quality, delivery, or price.
  • Stage stated once, confidently: building, not yet established.

Ambition, without pretending the work is already done.
