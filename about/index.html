A record before a network.

Udyog House is building a capability record for tool rooms, mould makers, and precision job-work — starting in one corridor, and only growing where the record has actually been built.

Starting in Vasai East; Waliv, Sativali, and Gokhivare next, when built. Vasai East, Palghar district, Maharashtra.

Who it is for

Two audiences, one record between them

For manufacturers, the record is a way to be discovered for what a shop can actually make — process, machine, material and grade, size, tolerance — in words the owner has approved, with the evidence and its limits shown beside each field. For buyers, the same record is a way to ask better questions before sharing a drawing, and to judge a claim by the check behind it rather than by the adjective in front of it.

The record comes first because everything else — discovery, comparison, conversation — depends on it being readable and honest. A network built on vague categories would only reproduce the problem it claims to solve.

Principles, as sentences

The site states what it knows and what it does not, in plain language, and it does not fill gaps with invented counts, members, testimonials, logos, partnerships, or revenue. Nothing here guarantees enquiries, orders, quality, delivery, or price. Information is published only with the owner's approval for that field.

Where it starts

Vasai East first, then only where the record is built

The record starts in Vasai East, Palghar district, Maharashtra, and grows only where it has genuinely been built — Waliv, Sativali, and Gokhivare next, when built. No other cluster is claimed in advance.

How it grows — planned, not live

Four phases, in order, none of them live yet

Each phase depends on the one before it being real. No phase is skipped by announcement.

  1. Phase 1 of 4 — Capability records

    Real, owner-approved capability records for tool rooms, mould makers, and precision job-work in Vasai East first.

  2. Phase 2 of 4 — Discovery by capability

    Finding shops by process, machine, material, size, tolerance, and evidence — only once real records exist to search.

  3. Phase 3 of 4 — Conversations, quotations, orders

    Structured conversations from a record, then quotations, comparison, and order milestones — only once discovery is real.

  4. Phase 4 of 4 — Wider corridors

    More clusters, only where the record has genuinely been built there. No cluster is claimed in advance.

Phase 3 raises an open decision: whether quotations run through Udyog House or direct between buyer and shop is not decided. Nothing in Phase 3 assumes an answer.

Why this record, why this corridor

The intent behind this project is simple — that a small workshop should be found for what it can actually make, and that a buyer should be able to tell, before sharing a drawing, whether a shop works in that material, at that tolerance, at all. The record is the way to make both of those things checkable, rather than a matter of who shouts loudest or who happens to be known already. Starting in one corridor, and doing that properly, matters more than claiming a network that does not exist yet.
