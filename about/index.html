About Udyog House

Founder-led and early-stage. Maharashtra beginning. Vision is separated from today, plainly, on this page.

Purpose

Udyog House is building India's industrial discovery and sourcing network, beginning in Maharashtra. Its purpose is to make industrial discovery and sourcing more structured, transparent, and accessible — starting with structured capability profiles for tool rooms, mould makers and precision machining businesses.

Principles

  • Exact, plain-spoken, rooted, builder-minded, fair.
  • Owner-approved information, with evidence and limits stated.
  • No invented counts, members, testimonials, logos, partnerships, or revenue.
  • No guarantee of enquiries, orders, quality, delivery, or price.
  • Stage stated once, confidently: building, not yet established.

Long-term vision — planned, not live

  1. Manufacturer Network — real, owner-approved capability profiles. Planned.
  2. Buyer Discovery Network — discover and filter by processes, machines, materials, dimensions, tolerances and evidence. Planned.
  3. RFQ, Matching & Order Room — buyer requirements, supplier invitations, quotations, comparisons, introductions and order milestones. Planned.
  4. National Industrial Infrastructure — expanded clusters, capability intelligence, reputation, integrations and workflows. Planned.

None of these phases is live on this staging site. Future clusters are only for when the network is genuinely built there.
