What this record still needs before anyone should rely on it

  • P1 — Document received: a named document, received and logged. Receipt alone is not verification.
  • P2 — Cross-checked: named fields matched against a named source, on a named date. Identity/document only — not capability or quality.
  • P3 — Observed capability: what was actually seen, where, by whom, by what method. Point in time only.
  • P4 — Transaction-evidenced: only that specific past transaction, by stated evidence. Never a rating or a future guarantee.

How it works today — honestly

Today, the work is the record. Not the platform.

01 → 03

Step 1 — Declare

Owner states it, in their own words

Process, machines, materials, dimensions, tolerances. Labelled P0 Supplier-declared. Nothing is upgraded by being written down.

Step 2 — Show the check

Evidence, with its limits attached

Document received, cross-checked, observed, or transaction-evidenced — each with scope, source, method, checker, date, and limits. See the ladder on Trust & Evidence.

Step 3 — Approve, then publish

Nothing goes live without the owner

No profile is published without owner approval for that field, and a correction or removal route must be live before any profile is ever published. Neither exists yet, because no profile exists yet.

Questions, answered plainly

Frequently asked questions

Can I search manufacturers on this site today?

No. There is no directory or search here today. The record builder above shows the shape of the record being built — it contains no shop and no list.

Does expressing interest guarantee enquiries or orders?

No. No enquiry, order, match, quality, delivery, or price outcome is guaranteed, on any page of this site.

Should I send drawings through the forms?

No. Do not send drawings or confidential specifications through this site. Describe the need in general terms first.

Is there a price for a profile?

No price is announced here. Any Free Basic / Paid Professional profile concept is unvalidated, and pricing is not announced.

Are the Privacy and Terms pages final?

No. They are drafts only and require qualified legal review. They make no compliance claim.

What happens after I submit a form?

On this preview site, nothing is sent. The form states clearly that no data has been sent and no network request was made.
