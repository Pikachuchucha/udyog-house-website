Discover manufacturers by what they can make.

Most industrial discovery fails at the same point: a shop says it “does CNC” — and nobody can tell what that covers.

Computer Numerical Control (CNC) is a category, not a capability. A useful record says more: the process, the machine and its travel, the material and its grade, the size, the tolerance and how it is measured — and the evidence behind each one, with its limits attached.

That record is what Udyog House is building, owner by owner, in one corridor first. Starting in Vasai East; Waliv, Sativali, and Gokhivare next, when built.

How to read this record

  1. 1 — Process, then machine. A process name alone is not a filter. The machine and its travel decide whether a job fits at all.
  2. 2 — Grade, not just material. Stainless steel alone cannot be quoted. SS 316, in a stated condition, with a certificate answer, can.
  3. 3 — Tolerance with a feature. ±0.02–0.05 mm means something only on a named feature, at a named size, with a named instrument.
  4. 4 — Evidence per field. The label belongs to one field, never to the business as a whole. A strong label on one field says nothing about the others.

How a record is built

Declare, check, approve — in that order

  1. 1

    Declare

    The owner states capability in their own words: process, machine, material and grade, size, tolerance, and what the shop does not do. That statement is labelled P0, supplier-declared, and nothing is upgraded by being written down.

  2. 2

    Check, with limits

    Each field can earn a higher label only with a stated check: a document received, fields cross-checked against a named source, capability observed on a named machine, or a past transaction evidenced. Every label carries its scope, method, checker, date, and limits. See Trust & Evidence.

  3. 3

    Approve, then publish

    You approve every field. Or it does not go up. Approval is recorded per field, and a correction and removal route must be live before any profile is ever published. Neither exists yet, because no profile exists yet.

Questions, answered plainly

Questions buyers and shops actually ask

What is a capability record?

A structured statement of what a shop can make: process, machine and travel, material and grade, size, tolerance with its feature and finish, inspection method — and the evidence behind each field, with its limits. One field, one label, no blanket claim.

How is this different from a directory?

A directory lists names and categories. A record answers the question a directory cannot: what this shop can actually make, at what tolerance, in what material, on what evidence. No directory exists here yet, and none is claimed.

Why start in Vasai East?

Because a record is only as good as its checks, and checks need a place: shops that can be visited, work that can be witnessed, and deliveries that can be made. Starting in Vasai East; Waliv, Sativali, and Gokhivare next, when built.

Can I search for a shop on this site today?

Not yet. There is no shop to search for here. What you can do today is build a draft record or check a brief, in your browser, and judge whether the record itself is worth your time.

What should I do before sharing a drawing?

Check the brief first: process, material and grade, tolerance band, quantity band, and the evidence you want to see. Share drawings only through a direct, agreed channel once a shop and a scope are clear — never through a preview form.
