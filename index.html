Why structured capability information matters

A buyer often needs to know, before sending a requirement, whether a shop works with a given process, machine, material, size range, or tolerance — and what evidence supports that. A manufacturer often needs to be found for that specific capability, not for a generic category. A structured record aims to hold those facts in one place, in the owner's own approved words, with the source and limits stated alongside each fact.

That record does not exist yet on this site. The blank template below shows the shape of the record being built. It contains no shop, machine, or data.

Starting place

Initial ecosystem: tool rooms, mould makers, mould-base manufacturers, CNC / VMC machining, precision engineering, industrial job-work, and adjacent manufacturing capabilities. Initial cluster: Vasai East / Waliv / Sativali / Gokhivare and the wider Mumbai industrial belt. Maharashtra first. Future clusters, including Bhosari, Pimpri-Chinchwad and Chakan, are only for when the network is genuinely built there — they are not claimed now.
