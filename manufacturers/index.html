Why capability visibility matters

Buyers look for a specific capability, not a generic category. A structured profile aims to state, in plain terms, what a business can make, with what processes and machines, in what materials and size ranges, and with what evidence — so the right conversation can start sooner, and the wrong one can be avoided earlier.

What a structured capability profile would contain

As a proposed record only, not a live profile:

  • Processes the business works with
  • Machines and equipment, in general terms
  • Materials worked with
  • Dimensions and tolerances, where relevant
  • Evidence label (P0 to P4) with scope, method, checker, date and limitations — see Trust & Evidence
  • Owner approval for every published field

Owner control and approval

Information in a profile would be owner-controlled. Nothing would be published without the owner's approval for that field, and a correction or removal route would need to be live before any profile is ever published. That route is not live on this staging site, because no profile is published here.

Basic and Professional — concept only

A Free Basic and Paid Professional profile concept may be explored in future. Pricing and paid conversion are not validated, and no price is announced on this staging site. No paid tier, checkout, or payment is offered here.

No guarantee

A profile, or an expression of interest, does not guarantee enquiries, orders, customers, or revenue. That limit is stated here plainly, and it applies to every page on this site.

Consent and confidentiality

Forms on this staging site ask for minimal information only, require separate unticked consent, and — as a staging placeholder — send no data at all. Do not send confidential drawings, GST documents, identity documents, or financial information.
