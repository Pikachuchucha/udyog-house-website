One question, answered three ways

What do I give, what do you do with it, what do I get back?

  1. What you give

    Statements about your own shop, in brief: processes, machines and travel, materials and grades, sizes, tolerances, and what you do not do. No drawings, no GST or identity documents, no financial information, no customer lists, no pricing — not through any public form on this site, and not as part of a draft record.

  2. What is done with it

    Under the proposed method, your statements are reviewed field by field, labelled with their evidence and limits, and shown back to you as a draft. Nothing is published, ranked, or scored from a draft. Payment, if ever introduced, will never upgrade an evidence label, improve search position, or add any trust badge.

  3. What you get back

    A record a buyer can actually read — and the right to approve, narrow, or withdraw every field of it. You approve every field. Or it does not go up. A record you have not approved does not exist in public, and a field you withdraw disappears rather than lingering as a placeholder.

What this record still needs before anyone should rely on it

What the evidence levels mean
  • P1 — Document received: a named document, received and logged. Receipt alone is not verification.
  • P2-ID — Identity cross-checked: identity or authorised representative only, matched to a named source. Proves nothing about capability.
  • P2-DOC — Document cross-checked: named document fields matched to a named source. About documents, not capability.
  • P3 — Observed capability: a named machine witnessed producing comparable work, measured by a stated method to a stated tolerance. Point in time only.
  • P4 — Transaction-evidenced: only that specific past transaction, by stated evidence. Never a rating or a future guarantee.

Missing from this draft

  • Machine envelope still missing: machine type and travel are the first real filters in job-work. A record without them cannot be matched to a job.
  • Grade still missing: nobody quotes a mould or a part in a generic material name. The grade is the quote — name it, or say it is not yet specified.

Proposed methodology — not yet operated

How a manufacturer would join, in six stages

Every stage below is labelled PROPOSED METHODOLOGY. No stage has been operated with a real manufacturer, and no part of it is a promise about timing, cost, or outcome.

Six proposed stages, in order
StageWhat happensStatus
Stage 1 of 6 — IntroductionYou agree to be considered, and name an authorised representative. No profile is created without that agreement, and no profile is ever scraped from the web or copied from a directory. A draft you decline or withdraw is deleted.Proposed
Stage 2 of 6 — Information collectionThe 13 fields below are collected briefly at first. Everything starts at P0, supplier-declared. You may withhold any field. Confidential drawings, GST, identity, financial, customer-list, pricing, and employee personal data are never collected through a public form.Proposed
Stage 3 of 6 — Documentation and reviewInformation is reviewed for consistency, completeness, and relevance to the field it supports. Labels are assigned per field, never to the business as a whole. The reviewer is never the supplier, and is not an accredited inspector. Outcomes are: approve, approve with narrowing, hold, or reject with a reason. You see the reviewed draft — wording, labels, limits, and dates — before Stage 4.Proposed
Stage 4 of 6 — Manufacturer approvalYou approve every field. Or it does not go up. Approval is recorded per field: approver name and role, date, and version. Approval is revocable, and it is not a warranty, an endorsement, or a transfer of ownership. Fields you refuse or partly approve are omitted entirely — never shown as “pending” placeholders. An edit by Udyog House to an approved field also requires fresh per-field approval before that field goes live again.Proposed
Stage 5 of 6 — PublicationOnly Stage-4-approved fields go live, each with its label, scope, method, checker, source, date, and limits shown beside it. No profile badge, score, ranking, or blanket claim appears. Full documents, GST, identity, financial information, drawings, customer names without consent, prices, and withheld fields are never published.Proposed
Stage 6 of 6 — MaintenanceYou may update, narrow, or withdraw your record at any time. Any edit to a labelled field drops that field to P0 until it is re-checked. A correction and removal route must be live before any publication, and stays live afterwards.Proposed

The 13 fields collected at Stage 2

  1. Business name
  2. Location area / locality (street address only with your explicit consent)
  3. Contact and authorised representative
  4. Processes
  5. Machines and equipment
  6. Materials
  7. Component / product categories
  8. Size and capacity bands
  9. Tolerance bands
  10. Technical capabilities, including limits and what the shop does not do
  11. Supporting documents, where appropriate
  12. Business-provided photographs, captioned as such, with consent, withdrawable
  13. Additional capability information

Document handling, in brief

Documents are logged by type and date, access-restricted, never published in full, and redacted where needed. A document is deleted after its check unless you consent to retention, for a period still to be decided. Anything sent despite the warning — a confidential drawing, an identity document, financial information — is deleted on receipt, not stored or published. Staffing that deletion route is still to be confirmed before any publication.

Commercial facts a record must eventually carry

Even a perfect capability record does not answer a buyer's commercial questions. Before any record is treated as complete, it must eventually carry: load and lead time, batch and repeat drift, inspection, rework, and scrap cost and who bears them, acceptance criteria, material sourcing (who supplies it), payment terms and milestones, and delivery or pickup. None of these is collected or published today.
