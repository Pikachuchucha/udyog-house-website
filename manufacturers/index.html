What this record still needs before anyone should rely on it

  • P1 — Document received: a named document, received and logged. Receipt alone is not verification.
  • P2 — Cross-checked: named fields matched against a named source, on a named date. Identity/document only — not capability or quality.
  • P3 — Observed capability: what was actually seen, where, by whom, by what method. Point in time only.
  • P4 — Transaction-evidenced: only that specific past transaction, by stated evidence. Never a rating or a future guarantee.
