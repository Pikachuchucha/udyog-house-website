Trust & Evidence — Planned — Definitions only

A label without its record is not used here.

Evidence labels are useful only when the reader can see exactly what was checked, by whom, when, and with what limits. No P0 to P4 label is applied to any manufacturer on this preview site. No record, profile, or evidence file is published here.

The ladder — read the limits, not just the rung

P0 to P4

Status is never shown by colour alone — every label is real text, with its full record fields.

P0 — Supplier-declaredDefinitions only — no label applied
Scope
The supplier's own words about its capability.
Source / method
Supplier statement only.
Checker
No independent checker is recorded for P0.

Date of the statement, when a record exists.

02

What is never claimed

No blanket claim. No shortcut to trust.

  • No blanket claim that a manufacturer is "verified", certified, accredited, approved, or vetted in general terms.
  • Udyog House is not an accredited inspection or certification body.
  • A factory visit, a GST document, or an identity check alone does not prove capability, quality, capacity, reliability, or delivery.
  • No pay-to-rank, pay-to-verify, or opaque trust score is offered or implied.
  • No ranking, testimonial, customer logo, partnership, transaction, or revenue claim is made on this site.
  • Expired or unrechecked labels must be downgraded or removed — silence is not an option, once labels exist. No label exists yet.

Correction and removal — future gate

A correction and removal route for published manufacturer information must be live before any profile is ever published. That route is not live on this preview site, because no profile is published here. The disputed-fact default — "under review" or immediate removal — is an open decision, not a preview claim.
